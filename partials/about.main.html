Who we are

A trusted name in dry fruit & agro-commodity trade

Deepak Mange & Co. is a well-established food processing and trading firm specializing in the import, processing, and distribution of premium edible nuts, with a strong focus on almonds.

Since our incorporation in 2018, we have earned a reputation for sourcing the finest dry fruits, maintaining strict quality standards, and ensuring efficient distribution across India and beyond. With our strategic presence in Navi Mumbai’s APMC Market, our success is driven by our commitment to quality, innovation, and customer satisfaction.
